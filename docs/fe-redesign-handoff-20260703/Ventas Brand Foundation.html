Think Biology.

The corporate identity ground for Ventas Bio — carried from the AmGraft® brand into the WorkOrder product. One scarlet, deep space, an editorial serif, and the language of regenerative science. Everything downstream inherits from here.

Brand essence

Precision, made to feel effortless.

Scientific, not clinical-cold

We are a regenerative-medicine company. The identity is exact and evidence-led, but warm and human — never sterile.

Depth as metaphor

"Dive to the science." Deep space, layered detail, a sense of going beneath the surface to where the biology happens.

Scarlet is a voice, not paint

The accent marks the brand and the critical — the logo, a selected indicator, a blocked badge. Everyday actions are ink. Kept rare, red stays loud when it must.

Color

Ink does the work, scarlet carries the brand

Everyday actions run on ink over near-black and paper-white. Scarlet is spent only where it counts — the mark and the critical. Scientific secondaries (ocean, green, amber) carry meaning in the product: status, not decoration.

Scarlet is a voice, not paint. The workhorse action color is ink (--primary #16161A) — buttons, focus, selected states. Scarlet lives in --brand (logo + a few deliberate accents) and in --destructive (critical only). Because red is now rare, a “Blocked” badge or a destructive action genuinely shouts. Never use scarlet as an ordinary button fill.

Iconography & imagery

Thin lines, deep photography

Icons are light, single-weight line marks. Imagery is real science — microscopy and the ocean-depth metaphor — treated as duotone in space and scarlet.

Line icons · 1.7px stroke · rounded caps

Match the product’s existing line set. Scarlet only for the active/selected state — never fill whole icons with it.

Imagery direction
Depth / ocean metaphor
SEM · duotone space
Tissue · scarlet duotone

Use real microscopy and lab photography. Grade to space-black + one accent (cyan for cool/scientific, scarlet for tissue). Keep marketing imagery out of dense product screens — reserve it for empty states and sign-in.
